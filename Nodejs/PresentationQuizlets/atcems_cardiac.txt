

Cardiac
Study online at https://quizlet.com/_jfk4q2
1. Systole is described as: Ventricle contraction, the pulmonic and aortic valve are open.
2. Diastole is described as: The heart relaxing/refilling. The tricuspid and bicuspid valve are open
3. Heart tissue gets fed during..: diastole
4. body tissue gets fed during..: systole
5. Coronary Sinus: an open space in the aorta for blood to flow. Attaches to coronary arteries that feed the 
heart
6. What is the SA node?: The primary pacemaker of the hearts ECS. beats at 60-100 bpm
7. What is the AV node?: The "bouncer" for ventricles. Beats at 40-60 bpm
8. What is the bundle of his?: The beginning of ventricular ECS. beats at 20-40 bpm.
9. What do the bundle branches beat at?: 20-40 bpm
10. What do the purkinje fibers beat at?: 20-40bpm
11. what ventricle has an abundance of perkinje fibers?: Left ventricle
12. What is depolarization?: electrical discharge triggering muscle contraction and pumping blood
13. What is repolarization?: Relaxation/recharging of the heart
14. What is a p wave on an EKG?: The SA node firing off
15. What is the space between the p wave and the QRS complex?: The av node
16. What is the qrs complex?: represents depolarization and contraction (bundle of his, bundle branches, 
perkinje fibers)
17. Frank sterlings law: stroke volume of the heart increases in response to an increase in the volume of 
blood filling the heart (cardiac output, stretch, and squish)
18. What are the 2 shockable rythms?: Vfib, pulseless Vtach
19. What is our bodies high pressure system?: Left side of the heart, aorta, arteries
20. What is our low pressure system?: Right side of the heart, vena cava, veins
21. Returning from the superior vena cava, where does our blood flow?: Through 
the right atrium, tricuspid valve, right ventricle, and pulmonic valve
22. Coming from the pulmonary artery, where does our blood flow?: Left atrium, 
bicuspid valve, left ventricle, aortic valve, aorta.
