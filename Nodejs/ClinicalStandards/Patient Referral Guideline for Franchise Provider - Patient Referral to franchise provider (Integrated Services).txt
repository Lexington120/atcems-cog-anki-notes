Standard:
To establish guidelines for referring individuals to franchise providers without OLMC consult.
Purpose:
To establish criteria for C4 referral to franchise provider without the requirement for OLMC consult
after MPDS triage and Integrated Services provider evaluation for efficient use of 9-1-1 resources.
If a patient does not meet these criteria but a franchise provider may still be appropriate, OLMC
should be consulted.
Application:
General Applicability:
1. Patient is coming from a skilled nursing facility
2.   Franchise provider has an ETA of ≤3 hours AND time is within next critical medication
administration if applicable
3. Patient stable per caller
Vital Sign Requirements:
1. Pulse: 60 – 110 bpm
2. Systolic BP: 90 – 200 mmHg
3. Respirations: 12 – 20 bpm
4. Blood Glucose: 70 – 300 mg/dL; no signs of DKA
5. SPO
2
≥ 93% on room air or prescribed supplemental oxygen
6. Temperature 96-100.4 degrees F
Criteria where OLMC is not required:
1. Lab abnormalities:
a. Hemoglobin (Hgb) greater than 5.5
i) Exclusions: known source of bleeding, symptomatic including shortness of
breath, increased fatigue, dizziness, chest pain
b. Sodium (Na) between 120-150
i)   Exclusions: AMS from baseline, nausea/vomiting, headache, seizure
c. Calcium (Ca) between 7.0-12
i) Exclusions: paresthesias, generalized weakness, AMS from baseline
d. Potassium (K) between 2.5 and 5.5
i)
Exclusions: generalized weakness, AMS from baseline
2. Scheduled or routine procedures:
a. Paracentesis, PICC line replacement, trach replacement
i. Exclusions: acute complaint pertaining to procedure such as abdominal
pain, concern for infection, difficulty breathing
3. Dislodged or clogged medical device
b. JP surgical drain
i. Exclusions: bleeding around site, concern for infection, severe pain from
baseline around site
