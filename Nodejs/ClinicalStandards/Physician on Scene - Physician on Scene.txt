Standard:
The medical direction of patient care at the scene of an emergency is the responsibility of those
most appropriately trained in providing such care. All care should be provided within the rules and
regulations of the Texas Medical Board of the State of Texas.
Purpose:
This standard is established to identify a chain of command for system providers when
encountering physicians on scene and to ensure the patient receives the maximum benefit of
appropriate physician resources.
Application:
The TMB has specific rules pertaining to the authority of a physician to order specific patient care
interventions on the scene of a medical call.
TAC §169.15requires that if the physician on scene is
not a system EMS physician, the following conditions must be met:
a) The physician should identify themselves appropriately with either a TMB wallet card or
other identifying device
b) Care must be appropriate for the circumstances
c) Care must be appropriately documented by the EMS agency including the physician’s
name
d) If possible, the physician on scene must be put in contact with and coordinate care with
OLMC
Providers should work within the COGs and assist the intervener physician to the benefit of the
patient. If any issues arise, contact OLMC.
